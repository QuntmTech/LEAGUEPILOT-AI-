# LEAGUEPILOT AI commercialization path

## Strongest initial offer

Sell **decision confidence and league entertainment**, not magical autopilot:

- Free: one league, lineup alerts, basic power rankings and rules-based reports.
- Pro: waiver plans, trade finder, AI explanations, scheduled Discord/GroupMe delivery.
- Commissioner: historical league hub, awards, branded recaps and member voting.

## Why this can differentiate

Most products optimize an individual roster or generate league content. This foundation connects the
two: team intelligence, an approval queue and a group-chat engagement layer using the same verified
snapshot and audit history.

## Launch gates

- Complete trademark, entity-name, domain and app-store research before public use of the locked
  LEAGUEPILOT AI name.
- Validate ESPN 2026 data mapping against at least three real league configurations.
- Replace the nflverse beta feed with a licensed provider whose contract permits commercial
  redistribution and whose SLA covers 90-minute game-day inactive updates.
- Run a private pilot for four NFL weeks and measure recommendation acceptance, data failures,
  notification engagement and AI cost per active league.
- Add Yahoo/Sleeper only after the normalized snapshot contract passes provider conformance tests.
- Complete the public-launch security items in `SECURITY.md`.
- Obtain legal review for ESPN terms, data use, privacy and marketing claims.

## Metrics worth tracking

- Weekly connected-league sync success rate.
- Recommendations viewed, approved and dismissed by kind.
- Median projected improvement accepted by users.
- Weekly report opens/clicks and league-member activation.
- AI cost and latency per active league.
- Credential refresh rate and ESPN schema-drift incidents.
